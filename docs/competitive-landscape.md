# Competitive landscape and differentiation

This is targeted product research, not a patent or freedom-to-operate opinion.

| Category | Existing capability | CommonsGate boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Queue-it | Pre-queue randomization, speed/volume mitigation and duplicate visitor controls | One human across multiple authorized delegates; policy-fact and agent-switch invariance |
| Cloudflare Waiting Room | FIFO/random visitor queueing based on sessions and cookies | Principal-level opportunity, human review, appeal and allocation proof |
| Selec | Deduplicated applications, seeded lotteries, deterministic results, offers, waitlists and audit trail | Delegated AI access, model/language normalization and counterfactual agent proof |
| Balanced Allocate | Batch constraint optimization and audit export | Live agent gateway, delegation and one-person opportunity budget |
| OpenID/AuthZEN | Approval, consent, delegation and agent/tool authorization patterns | Scarce-access policy, allocation and remedy layer that consumes identity assurance |

## Defensible claim

Do not claim novelty for lotteries, randomization, deduplication, waitlists or audit
logs. The differentiated system claim is:

> CommonsGate enforces and proves that changing only the delegated AI agent does
> not change a person's policy-relevant representation or allocation opportunity.

## Product wedge

Start with read-only shadow mode. A provider compares its existing queue against a
proposed agent-neutral charter without changing live outcomes. The report exposes
retry amplification, agent-tier outcome spread, language fallback, small-cell
suppression and exact agent-switch sensitivity. Live enforcement follows only
after the provider approves identity, policy, review and remedy boundaries.
