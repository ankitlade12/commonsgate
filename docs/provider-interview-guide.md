# Provider discovery interview guide

Do not pitch during the first ten minutes. Capture current workflow, constraints,
failure modes and language in the provider's own words. Do not request client data.

## Questions

1. What is the exact scarce unit: call-back, intake appointment, eligibility review,
   consultation or representation?
2. When demand exceeds capacity, how are requests ordered today?
3. Which facts legitimately affect urgency, and who is allowed to verify them?
4. What happens when two submissions appear to represent the same person?
5. How often do unanswered offers block capacity, and how is the waitlist advanced?
6. What errors can be appealed, who reviews them, and what remedy is operationally
   possible after a round closes?
7. Which languages and accessibility channels are currently hardest to support?
8. Would a read-only historical or synthetic shadow report be acceptable before a
   live integration? What evidence would make it useful?
9. Which systems hold identity, scheduling and case records? What integration is
   technically and contractually possible?
10. What would make an agent-access gateway unsafe or impossible to adopt?

## Evidence to record

- Role and organization type; publish a name only with explicit permission
- Current intake window, volume and capacity ranges
- Current queue/lottery/triage mechanism
- Review and remedy constraints
- Integration and procurement constraints
- Exact quote only with permission; otherwise paraphrase and label it as such
